Europe Project FAQ

Are CHANFER detergent packaging lines CE compliant?

Yes. CHANFER equipment is CE compliant and ISO 9001 certified, built with EU-standard electrical components (Siemens, Schneider, Festo, SMC), and can be supplied with documentation required for the European market.

Does CHANFER have local support in Europe?

CHANFER is establishing a European operation center in Milan, Italy, for local sales consultation, after-sales service coordination, and spare-parts logistics. Until it is fully operational, European clients receive direct support from our China-based team.

Which European markets does CHANFER serve?

CHANFER serves detergent and FMCG manufacturers across Europe, with particular focus on Italy, Germany, France, Spain, Poland, and the wider EU, plus the UK and CIS region.

Can CHANFER integrate with existing European factory lines?

Yes. CHANFER designs turnkey lines and individual machines that integrate with existing bottling, pouch, or drum production setups, with English/German-capable HMI and EU-standard safety and control systems.
