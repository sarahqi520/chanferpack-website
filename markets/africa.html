Africa Project FAQ

Which African countries has CHANFER served?

CHANFER has installed liquid detergent packaging lines in Nigeria, Egypt, Kenya, South Africa, and several other African markets, with 200+ production lines deployed across 40+ countries worldwide.

Do you provide installation and training in Africa?

Yes. CHANFER engineers travel for on-site installation (typically 15-30 days), commissioning, operator training, and trial production. Remote 24/7 technical support is included with every line.

What detergent container types are most popular in Africa?

Bottle (500ml-5L) and pouch (stand-up spout pouch) formats dominate African FMCG detergent markets, followed by drum/box for bulk and industrial cleaning products. CHANFER supplies complete lines for all three.

Can CHANFER equipment handle high temperatures and unstable voltage?

Yes. CHANFER lines are built with industrial-grade electrical components (Siemens, Schneider, Festo, SMC) and can be configured for local voltage and tropical operating conditions common across Africa.
